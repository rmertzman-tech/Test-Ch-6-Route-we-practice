The Avoided Conversation

Sam has a pattern. Whenever a serious conversation begins, Sam checks their phone, makes a joke, changes the subject, or suddenly remembers something else they need to do.

Sam says, “I just do not like drama.” Over time, friends stop bringing serious concerns to Sam. One friend says, “I like Sam, but I cannot trust them with hard things.”

Before learning the Chapter 6 tools, make your own first map.

Character ceiling: a repeated behavior may justify inquiry into formation. It does not automatically justify a global label about the whole person.

4 · LEARN — Four Instruments

See formation rather than labels

Virtue

A practiced excellence of character. Values become more available through practice.

Habit route

A repeated route from attention to action. Repetition can make a response easier and more automatic.

Practical wisdom

Ask what this concrete situation calls for rather than mechanically applying a label or rule.

Tested commitment

A claimed value becomes more informative when practiced under pressure, inconvenience, conflict, feedback, or repair.
